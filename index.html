Before you shop

Tips for Fantasy Cosplay

Four things that determine whether a costume reads from across the room or only close up.

01

Find the one recognizable piece first

Every character has a single element that makes people say the name from across a convention hall. Rhysand's violet eyes. Tamlin's gold mask. Lucien's mechanical eye. Mor's red dress. Build outward from that piece — it's the anchor everything else supports.

02

Series palette reads from a distance

If specific pieces aren't in budget, lean on the series color language. ACOTAR Night Court: black, purple, silver. Spring Court: forest green and gold. Crescent City: deep city blues and warm amber. Throne of Glass: ember gold and forest green. Fourth Wing: dark military brown and black with lightning accents. Color-coding works before anyone sees a prop.

03

Wings at conventions: plan ahead

Winged costumes are iconic but take significant space. At a crowded convention, articulating wings that fold flat are worth the extra cost. Check venue prop policies before you commit to a wingspan — some have limits. Fabric and foam wings are easier to manage than feathered ones in a crowd.

04

Ear tips make it fae

Silicone pointed ear tips are the single most cost-effective upgrade in ACOTAR cosplay. Applied with spirit gum, they sit on the outside of your own ear and stay put through a full convention day. Matched to your skin tone, they read effortlessly and photograph beautifully — a simple detail that transforms any look into fae royalty.
