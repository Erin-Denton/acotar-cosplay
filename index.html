Pick Your Character

Each guide shows the one piece people recognize at a distance, then builds from there. Difficulty ratings are for the full assembled look — the quick version of any character is always easier.

Feyre Archeron

High Lady · Night Court

Huntress, painter, High Lady of the Night Court. Feyre's look changes dramatically across the series — three distinct eras, each recognizable in its own right. Pick the version you love most.

Quick Look Easy

Black gown or flowing dark dress, star or constellation face paint on one cheekbone, silver jewelry. The face paint is the detail that reads as Night Court Feyre from across a convention floor.

👗Black maxi or evening dress→ ⭐Star & constellation face paint→ 💎Silver statement jewelry set→
High Lady Full Build Weekend project

Add a dramatic midnight cape, a star and crescent moon crown, and rhinestone gems to press along the cheekbone. A deep plum gown works if you want color over pure black.

👑Star & crescent moon crown→ 🧥Black / midnight blue cape→ ✦Celestial rhinestone face gems→ 🌙Deep plum formal gown (alt.)→
Spring Court Quick Look Easiest

Flowing sage green or blush dress, wildflower crown, and delicate leaf earrings. Feyre's Spring Court look is soft and fae — feminine with a restrained wildness to it.

🌿Sage green chiffon / flowy dress→ 🌸Wildflower floral crown→ 🍃Gold leaf & green earrings→ 👂Pointed fae ear tips (silicone)→
The Huntress Easiest

Brown or dark green faux-leather jacket, worn trousers, and boots. Feyre starts the story as a mortal hunter keeping her family from starving. Add a bow and quiver prop and it reads immediately.

🧥Brown faux-leather jacket→ 🏹Foam bow & quiver prop set→ 🥾Worn leather / combat boots→

Rhysand

High Lord · Night Court

High Lord of the Night Court. Violet eyes, dark hair, vast dark wings. Rhysand's look is unmistakably powerful — all controlled shadow and restrained danger. He dresses like he knows exactly how terrifying he is.

Quick Look Easy

All black: fitted black trousers, a high-collar black shirt or jacket. Add violet colored contacts if you want. The look alone reads as Rhysand to anyone who's read the books.

🖤Black high-collar dress shirt→ 👖Black slim-fit trousers→ 👁️Violet / amethyst colored contacts→
Full Wings Build Weekend project

Add wings, a long dramatic frock coat, and silver star jewelry. Wings can be large feathered costume wings painted matte black, or a harness worn under a cape that drapes like folded wings.

🦇Large black wings / wing harness→ 🧥Long black frock coat→ ✦Silver star & moon jewelry→ 💠Face rhinestone gems→
⚔️

Nesta Archeron

Valkyrie · Night Court

Feyre's fierce older sister. Turned fae against her will, she channels rage and grief into becoming a Valkyrie. The warrior look is silver, sharp, and proud.

Quick Look Easy

Silver or icy white dress with a structured warrior belt and silver arm cuffs. Nesta's power came from enchanted objects — silver metallic details throughout signal her story.

🥈Silver / icy white formal dress→ ⚔️Wide structured warrior belt→ 💪Silver arm cuffs / bracers→
Valkyrie Full Build Weekend project

Add the silver crown, a dramatic silver or white cape, and Athalar-style pointed fae ears. This is the Nesta who earned her Valkyrie title — cold-beautiful, unbreakable.

👑Silver leaf / warrior crown→ 🧥Silver / white dramatic cape→ 👂Pointed fae ear tips (silicone)→

Cassian

General · Night Court

General of Rhysand's armies and a warrior to his core. Battle-worn black leathers, massive wings, and war paint. He fights like he enjoys it, because he does.

Quick Look Easy

Black tactical jacket over a dark base, black combat boots, and war paint lines across the cheekbones and jaw. The face paint is Cassian's signature marker and takes five minutes with black face paint.

🧥Black tactical / field jacket→ 🥾Black combat boots→ 💄Black face paint (war paint)→
Full Warrior Build Weekend project

Add wings and faux leather armor panels or vest. A dark red bracer or sash adds the warrior camp color — brutal, imposing, ready for anything.

🦇Black wings / wing harness→ 🛡️Faux-leather armor vest→ 🔴Dark red leather bracer / sash→

Azriel

Spymaster · Night Court

Spymaster of the Night Court. Azriel moves like something darker than shadow — close-fitting matte blacks, a hooded cloak, and scarred hands always hidden in dark gloves. Quiet. Lethal. Watching.

Quick Look Easy

Black hooded cloak over all black, and dark leather gloves covering the hands and wrists. The gloves are the non-negotiable piece — Azriel's scarred hands are never uncovered. Keep everything muted, not dramatic.

🧥Black hooded cloak→ 🧤Black leather gloves→ ⚫Black fitted base outfit→
Spymaster Full Build Weekend project

Add a black-bladed knife prop and matte dark wings. Azriel's wings are darker and less prominent than Cassian's, so fabric wings in near-black charcoal work better than feathered ones here.

🗡️Black dagger prop→ 🦇Charcoal / matte black wings→ 💨Dark grey body / shadow paint→

Morrigan (Mor)

Third-in-Command · Night Court

The most powerful female warrior alive, Rhysand's Third-in-Command. Mor walks into a room and everyone knows it — usually in deep red or gold, devastatingly dressed for exactly the effect she intends.

The Red Dress Look Easy

Deep crimson bodycon or flowing red dress, gold jewelry stacked throughout, long golden blonde hair (wig if needed). The red dress is Mor's most recognized look — her armor for the political courts.

👗Deep red bodycon / formal dress→ ✨Gold layered jewelry set→ 👱Long golden blonde wig→ 💋Bold red lip color→
Full Build + Wings Weekend project

Mor can teleport, so she doesn't need armor — she shows up dressed for the occasion and then happens to have wings. Add wings and a gold hair accessory to complete the look.

🦋Large feathered wings→ 💛Gold hair comb / ornament→ 💄Gold / warm eyeshadow palette→

Lucien Vanserra

Emissary · Autumn Court

Son of the Autumn Court, emissary to the Spring Court. Auburn hair, a mechanical eye — a gift from Helion — and the kind of warm copper palette that makes him look like he walked out of a fae autumn. Sharp and a little sad.

Quick Look Easy

Auburn or copper wig (if needed), rust or amber tunic, and a steampunk-style mechanical eye patch or gear eye prosthetic. The eye is Lucien's most distinctive feature and the piece that makes the costume click.

🦊Auburn / copper-red wig→ 🟧Rust / amber tunic or vest→ ⚙️Steampunk mechanical eye patch→
Full Autumn Court Build Weekend project

Build out the Autumn Court palette in full: rust, amber, burnt orange, and deep gold. Add a cape in autumn-leaf shades, copper jewelry, and pointed fae ears. This is Lucien at his most court-recognizable.

🍂Autumn-toned cape / cloak→ ✨Copper & gold jewelry set→ 👂Pointed fae ear tips (silicone)→

Elain Archeron

Seer · Night Court

The gentlest Archeron sister. A Seer made fae, she tends her garden and sees visions of the future. Elain's look is soft, floral, and quietly ethereal — she belongs in a sunlit greenhouse, or somewhere beautiful and just slightly strange.

Quick Look Easiest

Soft floral dress in blush, ivory, or pale yellow, wildflower crown, and delicate gold jewelry. Elain is the most approachable of the three sisters to costume — she looks like a fairy-tale princess who spends mornings in the garden.

🌸Blush / floral midi or maxi dress→ 🌼Wildflower / meadow floral crown→ ✨Delicate gold chain jewelry→
Garden Seer Full Build Easy

Add a small wicker basket with faux flower stems as a prop, a soft linen apron layered over the dress, and subtle pointed fae ears. The Seer look keeps the beauty but adds a dreaming, faraway quality to it.

🧺Small wicker / rattan basket (prop)→ 🌷Faux flower stems (prop bouquet)→ 👂Subtle pointed ear tips→

Tamlin

High Lord · Spring Court

High Lord of the Spring Court. Blonde, green-eyed, and cursed to wear a mask by ancient magic. Tamlin's look combines the Spring Court's wild abundance with the gold mask that became one of the series' most iconic images.

Quick Look Easy

Forest-green tunic or vest over natural earth tones, and the gold leaf masquerade mask. The mask is what makes people say "Tamlin" — everything else supports it, but the mask is the costume.

🌲Forest green tunic / linen vest→ 🎭Gold leaf masquerade mask→ 🦌Antler headpiece / crown→
High Lord Full Build Weekend project

Add a dramatic forest-green hooded cloak, Spring Court gold accents throughout, and pointed fae ears. For his beast aspect, fur-trimmed layers add a wilder, more dangerous edge to the nature-king look.

🧥Forest green hooded cloak→ ✨Gold & green jewelry / belt→ 👂Pointed fae ear tips→

Before you shop

Tips for ACOTAR Cosplay

Four questions that determine which direction to build from.

01

Find the one recognizable piece first

Every character has a single element that makes people say the name from across a convention hall. Rhysand's violet eyes. Tamlin's gold mask. Lucien's mechanical eye. Mor's red dress. Build outward from that piece — it's the anchor everything else supports.

02

Court color reads from a distance

If the specific pieces aren't in budget, lean on court palette: Night Court characters wear deep black, purple, and silver. Spring Court runs in forest greens and spring gold. Autumn Court is copper, rust, and amber. Color-coding reads before anyone sees a prop or detail.

03

Wings at conventions: plan ahead

Winged costumes are iconic but take significant space. At a crowded convention, articulating wings that fold flat are worth the extra cost. Check venue prop policies before you commit to a wingspan — some have limits. Fabric and foam wings are easier to manage than feathered ones in a crowd.

04

Ear tips make it fae

Silicone pointed ear tips are the single most cost-effective upgrade in ACOTAR cosplay. Applied with spirit gum, they sit on the outside of your own ear and stay put through a full convention day. Matched to your skin tone, they read effortlessly and photograph beautifully — a simple detail that transforms any look into fae royalty.
