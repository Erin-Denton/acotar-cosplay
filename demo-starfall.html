The Night Court Sky

Click anywhere on the page to shoot a star. Every 20 seconds a Starfall shower fills the sky. Links and buttons still work normally.

✦ Click the night sky to shoot a star ✦
🌹

Ambient Starfield

150–200 stars across three depth layers with sine-wave twinkle (3–8 s per cycle). Bright near-layer stars occasionally flash a four-point sparkle glint.

🖱️

Cursor Parallax

Move your mouse across the screen — far stars drift 1 px, near stars up to 6 px. Stars within 120 px of the cursor brighten by 20%.

💫

Click-to-Shoot

Tap or click anywhere (except links & buttons) to fire a shooting star through that exact point. Up to 3 can streak at once; nearby stars flare in the wake.

🌠

Starfall Event

Every 20 s, 10–16 stars shower across the upper sky in a shared direction. The sky brightens with a violet wash, then ends in a sparkle wave.

Most-Tweaked CONFIG Values

  • STARFALL_INTERVAL — seconds between showers. Default 20; set lower to see events faster.
  • STAR_COUNT_BASE — stars at 1920×1080. Scales by screen area; raise for denser skies.
  • TWINKLE_PERIOD_MIN / MAX — how slowly stars breathe (seconds). Wider range = more variety.
  • PARALLAX_NEAR — max px shift for front-layer stars. 0 disables parallax entirely.
  • SHOOT_MAX — concurrent shooting stars on click. Set to 0 to disable click-to-shoot.
  • NEBULA_OPACITY — corner haze intensity. 0 removes the nebula.
  • BG_TOP / BG_BOTTOM — the gradient stops of the sky itself.
